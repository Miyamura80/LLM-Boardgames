Clue by clue

Every turn: the spymaster's one-word clue and its number, then each guess with what the card turned out to be. A guess on your own agent continues the turn; a bystander or an enemy agent hands the table over; the assassin ends the game on the spot. 💭 entries are the model's private reasoning for that decision — observability only, never fed back into play.
